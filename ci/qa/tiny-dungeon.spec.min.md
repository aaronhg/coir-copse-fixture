# Tiny Dungeon — Design Spec (minimal)

A one-screen, turn-based dungeon crawler. Tap Attack to fight monsters; clearing them takes the hero
deeper.

## Controls

- **Attack** — the hero attacks the current monster.
- **Flee** — abandon the current encounter; a **new encounter begins** with a fresh monster.
- **Menu (gear)** — opens the settings panel; the **X inside closes it** (the X must be usable
  whenever the panel is open).
- **Restart** — appears on game-over; starts a fresh run.

## HUD

- **HP**, **Enemy HP**, **Defeated** (kills this run), and **Floor** (current depth) are shown on screen.
